Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum)

Early Blight

Pathogen: Alternaria solani
Moderate Severity
AI Detection Confidence 96.8%

Identified Symptoms

Concentric dark rings (target-like spots) with surrounding yellow chlorotic halos on lower mature foliage. Early stem lesions detected.

Recommended Treatment & Action

Organic & Cultural Care
  • Prune lower infected foliage and dispose safely off-site.
  • Apply copper-based bio-fungicide or Bacillus subtilis spray.
  • Avoid overhead sprinkler watering; keep leaves dry.
Targeted Intervention
  • Apply Chlorothalonil or Mancozeb preventative spray at 7-10 day intervals.
  • Rotate fungicide classes (FRAC group 7 & 11) to mitigate resistance.

Best Practices

Plant Health & Disease Prevention Guide

Key agronomic guidelines to maximize crop resilience against common foliar pathogens.

Smart Drip Irrigation

Water roots directly rather than overhead foliage. Fungal spores (like *Phytophthora* and *Alternaria*) require 4-6 hours of continuous leaf wetness to germinate.

Airflow & Canopy Spacing

Maintain recommended row spacing and prune lower sucker leaves to enhance air circulation and lower localized microclimate humidity below 70%.

Soil Nutrient Balance

Avoid excessive Nitrogen which promotes lush, soft foliage susceptible to rust and powdery mildew. Balance with Potassium (K) and Calcium (Ca) for tough cell walls.
